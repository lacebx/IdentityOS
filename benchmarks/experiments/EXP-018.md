## Hypothesis

Adding a lightweight self-consistency verification step that re-prompts the model with its own answer and asks it to confirm or correct the final numeric/result before returning should reduce arithmetic and tool-call errors without adding much latency.

## Change

Modify OllamaAdapter.process to run a short verification pass on the final answer when the task appears to require a concrete result (e.g., contains arithmetic or tool-like keywords), and only replace the answer if the model provides a corrected one.
