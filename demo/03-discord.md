## Step 3: Discord

Someone in your Discord server types:

> "@ArseneBot what is Arsene working on?"

**The bot responds:**

> *He's preparing for a move to Tokyo:*
> *- Resume is 56% complete*
> *- Apartment search (Shibuya) hasn't started*
> *- Japanese study is overdue*
> *- Budget: $1,800/month*
>
> *Check in with him — he needs encouragement on the resume.*

The same identity. New workspace. No setup. No configuration.

The identity isn't tied to ChatGPT. It isn't tied to Grok. It isn't tied to Discord.

It simply *appears* wherever you are.
