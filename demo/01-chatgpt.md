## Step 1: ChatGPT

Imagine you're sitting at your desk. You open ChatGPT.

You type:

> "I'm moving to Tokyo next month. I have a $1,800/month budget. Help me find
> backend engineering jobs. I want to learn Japanese. Keep me accountable."

The AI responds. But underneath, something different happens:

- A **goal** forms: "Move to Tokyo" — critical, lifelong
- **Intentions** track: Resume, Apartment search, Japanese study
- Your **facts** are stored: budget, role, neighborhood preference, timeline
- **Evidence** links every detail back to your exact words
- **Timeline** records each commitment

You close ChatGPT.

Nothing feels different. That's the point.
