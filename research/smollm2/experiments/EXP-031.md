# Experiment EXP-031

**Status:** IN PROGRESS (as of 2026-08-24 ~08:00 UTC)

## Hypothesis

Adding a concise, task-agnostic "verify your final answer" instruction to the system prompt
should reduce arithmetic and factual slips in A05, C01, C02, E01, E02, E03 without adding
latency or hurting truthfulness.

## Bottleneck

Third attempt at the verify-directive approach. EXP-029 and EXP-030 both hurt tool-use
(tools dropped from 3/5 to 1/5). This iteration explores whether a differently phrased
or positioned verification instruction avoids disrupting tool-call formatting.

## Change

with a short verification directive (autopilot-generated variant of EXP-029/030).

Pytest target: `tests/test_adapters.py`

## Expected mechanism

Same hypothesis as EXP-029/030. If this variant avoids breaking tool-call structure, it
may recover the reasoning gains without the tool regression.
