## Failure analysis

The verify-directive completely collapsed the long_task category (2/5 → 0/5).
E04 (plan-then-execute write-up), which was previously passing, now fails.
A03 (numeric comparison) also regressed — was PASS in EXP-026, FAIL here.

**Confirmed pattern across EXP-029/030/031:** Any system prompt addition that asks
the model to "verify" or "double-check" disrupts SmolLM2-360M's ability to follow
structured task instructions. The model appears to prioritise the meta-instruction
over the task itself, particularly for multi-step and structured-output tasks.

This is a **model capability limit**, not a tuning problem: SmolLM2-360M cannot
reliably follow two competing instruction types simultaneously (task + verification).
